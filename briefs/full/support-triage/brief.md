# Ticket Triage Assistant

Our support team (8 agents) handles around 400 tickets a day for a B2B invoicing product. Tickets land in a shared inbox. Every morning a team lead reads through them, tags them, and assigns them out. It takes about 90 minutes, and things still slip through.

We'd like a tool that helps triage incoming tickets. We have historical tickets you can work from, or you can generate realistic sample data.

You may use an LLM API (OpenAI or Claude keys available).
