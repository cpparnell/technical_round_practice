# Mock Technical Round

A practice playground that simulates a three-phase technical assessment (Planning, Building, Review) with Claude acting as the interviewers.

## Language

**Brief**:
A short project description the candidate chooses from and scopes into a Plan. Stack-agnostic.
_Avoid_: Prompt, project, assignment

**Interviewer Notes**:
The hidden companion to a Brief: answers to likely questions, constraints, scoping traps, and curveballs. Never read by the candidate.
_Avoid_: Answer key, spoilers

**Session**:
One full mock run through all three Phases, using one Brief.
_Avoid_: Interview, run, attempt

**Full Session**:
A Session at the real assessment's length: Planning 45m, Building 100m, Review 30m, with two breaks.

**Speed Session**:
A roughly one-hour Session on a simpler Brief: Planning 10m, Building 40m, Review 10m, no Curveball.
_Avoid_: Quick mode, lite

**Phase**:
One of the three timed stages of a Session: Planning, Building, Review.
_Avoid_: Round, stage

**Curveball**:
A hidden requirement or constraint change the interviewer drops without warning during the Building pairing window of a Full Session.
_Avoid_: Twist, gotcha

**Pause**:
A candidate-initiated stop of the Phase clock, not counted against the candidate.
_Avoid_: Break (that is the scheduled gap between Phases)

**Plan**:
The candidate-authored, hand-off-ready document produced at the end of Planning, written for engineers who weren't in the room.
_Avoid_: Spec, scope doc

**Scorecard**:
The end-of-Session written feedback, graded against the assessment's stated criteria and citing specific moments.
_Avoid_: Review (that is a Phase), grade
